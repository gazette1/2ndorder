# SBFG: Avoid; water shortage is a tail risk that the current filings do not make shortable

## Bear case first

The short fails on the current record. SBFG shows nine consecutive quarters of loan growth [0], stable credit results [1], and an allowance management calls adequate [6]. Agricultural and commercial business loans are only 16.1% of the held-for-investment book [4], and a significant portion of the portfolio is real-property-secured [3]. No cited filing mentions water rights or water risk. Insider selling is small, $35.6K net (Form 4), no customer is named, and federal awards are $0 (USASpending). A short needs a credit catalyst; the filings show none.

## Business today

State Bank provides consumer and small business financial services, wealth management, private client services, mortgage banking, and commercial and agricultural lending from 27 offices in Ohio and northeast Indiana [2]. Average earning assets were $1.40B in 2025, up from $1.31B in 2024 [7]. Cash was $149.5M and revenue was not reported (SEC XBRL). The Company sold $250.4M of originated mortgages [8] and $1.0M of non-mortgage small business and farm credits in 2025 [9].

## Chain position

SBFG sits at the farm credit and agricultural lenders node of the water-shortage map, at risk with a mid horizon. The economic logic is that farm cash flow deterioration raises delinquencies and lowers irrigated land values if water rights are curtailed. That risk is inferred from the 16.1% agricultural and commercial business loan mix [4], not from any disclosed water-related initiative.

## Customer graph

The dossier customer graph shows no named customers, no other filers mentioning the company, and $0 in federal awards (USASpending). The real payors are bank borrowers and depositors, but the evidence does not name them. Enterprise and government buyer categories are empty; the customer base is unproven from the excerpts.

## Insider and management signal

Trailing twelve-month open-market insider activity is net selling of $35.6K, with one buy and two sales by one insider (Form 4). That is a minor negative, not a conviction-sized signal. Management language stresses credit stability, nonperforming asset progress [1], and allowance adequacy [6], with no mention of water shortage.

## Street view

Sell-side aggregators show unknown analysts, no consensus, and no mean target (Street view). No firm rating action is cited. Thin coverage is part of the avoid case, not a source of near-term pressure.

## Non-linear case

If water rights are curtailed in SBFG's Ohio and Indiana footprint, farm cash flow falls, the 16.1% agricultural and commercial business loan book [4] produces delinquencies, and real-property collateral [3] loses value because farmland appraisals embed water access. The allowance [6] would then be understated. The nine-quarter expansion [0] and NPA progress [1] would reverse. For this to compound into a large downside move, water stress must show up in SBFG's own credit metrics; the filing record contains no dated water event.

## Evidence

- [0] 8-K: ninth consecutive quarter of loan portfolio expansion.
- [1] 8-K: credit results show stability and progress resolving nonperforming assets.
- [2] 8-K: State Bank offers consumer and small business services, wealth management, private client, mortgage, and commercial and agricultural lending through 27 offices.
- [3] 10-K: a significant portion of the loan portfolio is secured by real property.
- [4] 10-K: commercial business and agricultural loans were 16.1% of the HFI loan portfolio; commercial real estate loans were 50.6%.
- [6] 10-K: allowance is adequate for currently anticipated losses based on past experience, economic conditions, borrower situations, collateral values, and other factors.
- [7] 10-K: average earning assets increased to $1.40B in 2025 from $1.31B in 2024.
- [8] 10-K: sold $250.4M of originated mortgages in 2025.
- [9] 10-K: non-mortgage loan sales were $1.0M, with a gain of $0.14M.

## Sizing and survivability

Average daily dollar volume is $965.8K, so building a position would take about 254 trading days (Reality check). Net cash is $148.9M and the share count fell 4.2% in twelve months (Reality check). Low volume is the main threat to an active short; cash runway cannot be estimated because revenue is not reported (SEC XBRL).

## What would change our mind

A 10-Q or 10-K showing rising agricultural delinquencies, higher allowance expense, or more nonperforming assets would turn this from avoid into a short. A Form 4 with sustained multi-insider buying would weaken the negative insider signal. An 8-K or call transcript that names water rights as a credit factor would create a dated catalyst.
