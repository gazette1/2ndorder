# LNN: Irrigation software is the water-shortage beneficiary, but the filing does not prove the recurring revenue thesis

## Bear case first
The filing is a negative read for the software theme. Irrigation revenue fell 7% and management blames soft North American sentiment [1][4]. The company already sells FieldNET and FieldWise [14], so this is not new optionality, and no software revenue, growth, or investment plan is disclosed. Insider trading is net zero, and customer evidence is mostly federal awards that are not clearly tied to irrigation. The MENA backlog is equipment revenue, not a recurring software catalyst [9][12].

## Business today
Lindsay is a manufacturer and distributor of irrigation and infrastructure equipment and technology [0][13]. In Q3 FY2026, irrigation revenue was $133.0MM, down 7% from $143.7MM [4]; infrastructure revenue rose but was offset by the irrigation decline [2]. Dossier fundamentals show total revenue $659.1MM and cash $250.6MM as of 2025-08-31 (XBRL).

## Chain position
Lindsay occupies the order-2 beneficiary node: installed irrigation hardware creates demand for software that converts sensor readings into irrigation prescriptions. FieldNET Advisor is listed as scheduling technology [14], so the software already exists but sits downstream of equipment sales. The economic logic is recurring subscriptions after hardware installation, and the filing does not quantify that path.

## Customer graph

Unproven: agricultural software customers, because no named growers or subscription revenue are disclosed. ## Insider and management signal
Form 4 shows no open-market insider buying or selling in the trailing 12 months, net $0 by 0 insiders (Form 4). Management language focuses on market softness, commodity pressure, and MENA project delivery [1][10][12], not on software strategy. There is no insider conviction signal, positive or negative.

## Street view
The supplied data show no visible sell-side coverage: unknown analyst count, consensus n/a, mean target n/a. No recent firm rating actions are available. Thin coverage means the software narrative has not been publicly stress-tested.

## Non-linear case
A non-linear outcome requires recurring software revenue to emerge inside the irrigation segment. If growers pay annual subscriptions for FieldNET or FieldNET Advisor across a meaningful share of the installed base, revenue quality could shift from equipment-cycle to software-like, and the multiple could expand. The filing discloses no software metrics, so this remains an open question. One-time MENA equipment revenue [12] does not build the recurring case.

## Evidence
- Fiscal 2026 third quarter results were reported in an 8-K filed 2026-07-02 [0].
- North America irrigation demand is tempered by margin pressure from high input costs [1].
- Infrastructure revenue increase was offset by the irrigation revenue decrease [2].
- Irrigation segment revenue was $133.0MM, down 7% from $143.7MM [4].
- Irrigation backlog increased due to the MENA project while infrastructure backlog decreased [9].
- Management cites soft U.S. conditions and continued MENA delivery [10].
- Management expects about $70MM of MENA project revenue in the current fiscal year [12].
- Lindsay is a manufacturer and distributor of irrigation and infrastructure equipment and technology [13].
- FieldNET, FieldWise, and FieldNET Advisor are listed as irrigation technology brands [14].

## Sizing and survivability
A small fund can own this on liquidity: $20.4MM average daily dollar volume means about 2 trading days to build a position (reality check). Net cash is $135.3MM against the $659.1MM revenue base (XBRL). Share count fell 6.4% in 12 months, so dilution is not an active threat (reality check). The unresolved threat is the absence of software revenue disclosure.

## What would change our mind
- A 10-Q or 10-K that breaks out software or subscription revenue and customer counts.
- A Form 4 showing open-market insider buying.
- A USASpending federal award specifically for FieldNET or irrigation software, not equipment.
