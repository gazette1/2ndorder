# LMNR: Short or avoid; water scarcity is a margin eliminator, and one-time water asset sales mask a shrinking farming base

## Bear case first
Ventura County was free from general drought conditions as of April 30, 2026 [12], and LMNR holds about 8,500 acre-feet of Santa Paula Basin rights and 11,500 acre-feet of Class 3 Colorado River rights [10][11]. Water sales have produced gains at $30,000 per acre-foot [2], and insiders are net buyers at $253M (Form 4), though 5 open-market sales show mixed conviction. Filings show no named customers or federal awards, so revenue attribution is unproven (USASpending). The short thesis needs drought to intensify; the current filing says the opposite for California [12].

## Business today
LMNR sells orchard crops and water rights from California and Arizona [7][10][16]. Revenue is $153.7MM and cash is $1.5MM as of 2025-10-31 (SEC XBRL). It recorded water rights gains in Q1 FY2025 [3][5] and agreed to sell Chilean properties for $15.0MM [0][16], but reported no water rights sales in FY2026 H1 [19].

## Chain position
LMNR is the first node in the at-risk irrigated agriculture chain. Water is a fixed input with few short-run substitutes, so a small allocation shortfall eliminates margins on marginal acres. Downstream processors see volume declines only after LMNR's throughput falls.

## Customer graph
Customer evidence is thin. The only named buyer is San Pedro, SpA, an enterprise counterparty in the Chile property sale [0]; no named enterprise buyers for farming output. Federal award dollars are $0 and one other filer names LMNR (USASpending), so farming revenue is unproven as to who pays.

## Insider and management signal
Insiders are net open-market buyers at $253M across 3 buys and 5 sales by 2 insiders (Form 4). Management language is adaptive: a plan to monetize Class 3 water rights and substitute low-water crops [1][15], with no quantified targets or dedicated capital.

## Street view
Sell-side coverage is absent: no analyst count, consensus, or mean target. For a $235MM name with no Street view, neither longs nor shorts can rely on analyst catalysts.

## Non-linear case
The downside compounds if drought returns and Colorado River allocations tighten. Class 3 rights [11] shift from monetization asset to compliance cost, fallowing agreements extend without prepaid proceeds [14], and water rights sales stop, with none in FY2026 H1 [19]. One quarter of cash runway and negative net cash (Reality check) force a going-concern filing or dilutive raise. Each acre-foot sold removes water and future revenue [2][10].

## Evidence
- Chile sale to San Pedro, SpA for $14,967,000 [0].
- Plan to monetize Class 3 Colorado River rights via crop substitution [1][15].
- Santa Paula Basin water rights sold at $30,000 per acre-foot [2].
- Water and pumping rights sales: $1,440,000 and $300,000 prices; $1,200,000 and $288,000 gains [3][5].
- Water resources include rights, usage rights, and pumping rights under aquifers and canals [7].
- About 8,500 acre-feet in Santa Paula Basin and about 11,500 acre-feet of Class 3 Colorado River rights [10][11].
- Ventura County free from general drought conditions as of April 30, 2026 [12].
- Fallowing agreements in FY2022 and FY2023, extended through calendar 2026 [14].
- Chile sale includes 500 acres lemons, 100 acres oranges, unplanted lands, water rights for $15.0 million [16].
- No water rights sales in the first six months of FY2026 [19].

## Sizing and survivability
A small fund needs about 28 trading days to build a position at $1.2MM average daily dollar volume (Reality check). Net cash is -$71.0MM and runway is about one quarter (Reality check), which threatens a long thesis more than a short. Share count rose 0.3% in 12 months (Reality check), a modest dilution drag.

## What would change our mind
- A 10-Q with multi-year fallowing or water supply agreements and prepaid proceeds would extend runway and weaken the short.
- A Form 4 with significant insider buying would strengthen the protective read.
- A named customer or federal award in USASpending would establish revenue durability.
