## Non-linear case
The path to a multiple of today's business requires drought to become a recurring condition in major growing regions, not a single dry year. If growers reclassify these inputs as necessary, FMC can attach higher-margin biologicals and seed treatments [1] to its base chemistry. The pipeline claim [9] allows for new stress-management products, but the excerpts name none. Multi-year contracts would make revenues less seasonal and more visible, supporting a higher valuation. The current evidence does not show that transition.
