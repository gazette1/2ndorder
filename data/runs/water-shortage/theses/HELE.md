# HELE: Consumer filter litigation, not a desalination component winner

## Bear case first
The filings point to patent litigation on PUR gravity-fed water filtration systems, not to a water-shortage opportunity [0][1][2][3]. There is no quantified theme revenue, no dated business catalyst, no management commitment, and no customer evidence tied to desalination or advanced treatment. The provided composite score is 8/100, with zero in optionality, revenue-to-opportunity, catalyst density, and management conviction. The bear reading is that HELE is a consumer filtration brand whose water-shortage relevance is limited to defending existing filter sales against infringement claims. Zero insider buying over the trailing 12 months removes one bull signal, and the negative net cash position adds risk (Form 4, reality check).

## Business today
From the evidence, HELE sells PUR gravity-fed water filtration systems that are the subject of patent infringement litigation and an ITC action [0][1][2]. Revenue is $1,786.3MM and cash is $18.9MM as of 2026-02-28 (SEC XBRL). The excerpts do not describe sales to water utilities, project developers, or component buyers.

## Chain position
The seed thesis places HELE as a membrane and filtration component supplier at order 3 in the adoption chain. The economic logic for that node is front-loaded component demand during desalination or reuse construction and a replacement tail afterward. The filing evidence does not place HELE in that node, because the named systems are end-consumer filters rather than components for treatment projects [2].

The filings contain no such segment, no capital expenditure, no design win, and no named project [0][1][2][3]. The non-linear case hinges on undisclosed plans that are absent from the evidence.
