In addition, the Board of Directors may submit other matters to the stockholders for action at the Annual Meeting. Our stockholders also may submit proposals for inclusion in the proxy material. In addition, our bylaws provide that a stockholder may present from the floor a proposal that is not included in the proxy statement if the stockholder delivers written notice to our Corporate Secretary not earlier than 120 days and not later than 90 days before the first anniversary of the preceding year s annual meeting.
