## Business today
FLNC sells grid-scale energy storage assets positioned as a key solution to power grid capacity constraints [0]. Revenue was $2.26B and cash was $690.8M as of 2025-09-30 (SEC XBRL). The available excerpts do not name specific customer segments beyond storage for grids.

## Chain position
FLNC occupies the order 3 node: a manufacturer that shifted to domestic suppliers and now faces capacity shortages and longer lead times. The rush to a small pool of qualified domestic suppliers gives those suppliers pricing power, transferring margin back to them. The equilibrium price rises until it approaches the tariff-inclusive import price, eroding the benefit of the shift for FLNC.

## Non-linear case
The downside compounds if tariff escalation raises imported component costs while domestic supplier bottlenecks delay deliveries. FLNC would absorb both input cost inflation and late-delivery penalties on fixed-price contracts, and the filings do not show pass-through clauses. If project prepayments slow and revenue falls faster than operating costs, the 19-quarter cash runway shortens. The multiple in this scenario is a downside multiple: the stock could reprice toward a fraction of current market cap, with the $690.8M cash pile as the only floor.
