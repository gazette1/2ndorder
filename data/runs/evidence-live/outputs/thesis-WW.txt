# WW: Avoid on restructuring distress, not API reshoring, with zero thematic exposure

## Bear case first
The theme-driven short thesis fails on relevance: WW sells weight loss services, not pharmaceutical ingredients, so the API reshoring mechanism does not apply. The restructuring support agreement [5] gives WW a negotiated path with creditors, and $160M cash (SEC XBRL) funds operations during the process. The dossier shows zero insider open-market activity (Form 4) and no documented customer concentration risk, though the customer base is unproven. The filings do not show any API-related exposure, leaving a reshoring short without a fundamental hook.

## Business today
WW reports revenue of $347M and cash of $160M (SEC XBRL) from its weight loss services business. The 8-K shows WW, as a company party, entering a restructuring support agreement dated May 6, 2025 [5]. The agreement states it is not an offer or acceptance of a Chapter 11 plan [0]. It contemplates a new credit agreement [11] and a new notes indenture [12], with definitive documents to be consistent with the terms [18].

## Non-linear case

If the restructuring support agreement [5] collapses and milestones [10] are missed, a non-consensual Chapter 11 filing likely leaves common equity worthless. Net debt of $1.27B (SEC XBRL) against a $163M market cap means any distressed debt-to-equity conversion would dilute current holders to zero. Operating losses would accelerate cash depletion from the $160M balance (SEC XBRL) if the restructuring drags on.
