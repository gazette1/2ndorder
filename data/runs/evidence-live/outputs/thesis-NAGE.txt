# NAGE: FDA-authorized Niagen supply to 503B compounding is the verified business; reshoring-driven domestic sourcing is absent from filings

## Bear case first
The reshoring thesis fails on the filings: the only tariff reference is a macro risk factor, not a strategy [18]. No capex or capacity expansion appears in the filings. The policy channel has no proven payer: no named customers (customer graph) and no federal awards (USASpending). Insider buying of $45.6K (Form 4) is negligible against a $273M market cap. The regulatory moat is real [11], but it protects a compliance business, not a reshoring winner.

## Business today
NAGE sells nicotinamide riboside (Niagen) in food-grade and pharmaceutical-grade forms [11][9]. The FDA has authorized pharmaceutical-grade Niagen for compounding by 503B outsourcing facilities, which make Niagen IV and injectable formulations for clinics [11][5]. Revenue was $129.4M and cash was $44.5M as of 2025-12-31 (SEC XBRL); distribution runs through 503B facilities and compound pharmacies abroad [1][7].

## Chain position
NAGE sits at the ingredient-supplier node, upstream of compounding pharmacies and clinics. Its economic logic is regulatory: FDA authorization [11] and dietary ingredient notifications [9][10] create a legal moat. The position is valid, but it is a compliance-driven supplier relationship, not a platform with pricing power.

## Customer graph
Unproven. No named enterprise customers appear in the filing excerpts (customer graph), and federal awards are $0 (USASpending). Six other filers name the company (customer graph), but actual buyers are undisclosed 503B facilities and compound pharmacies [1][5]. Revenue exists, but the customer graph is thin.

## Insider and management signal
One insider bought $45.6K net over the trailing 12 months, with 2 buys and 0 sales (Form 4). Directionally positive but small. Management language is compliance focused: commitment to FDA regulations [14] and regulatory oversight [12], with no reshoring targets.

## Street view
Coverage is zero: analyst count unknown, consensus n/a, mean target n/a (street view). A $273M company with $129M revenue and no sell-side coverage is under-covered. Thin coverage matters only if the thesis has filing support, which this one lacks.

## Non-linear case
A multiple requires a federal or state mandate for domestic pharmaceutical ingredient sourcing, forcing 503B facilities to sign long-term agreements with NAGE. Tariffs, which the 10-K lists as a risk factor [18], could raise the landed cost of foreign Niagen and give pricing headroom. That outcome requires named customers, capacity additions, and policy-driven demand in filings. None are present today.

## Evidence
- FDA has authorized pharmaceutical-grade Niagen for compounding by 503B outsourcing facilities [11].
- 503B facilities may compound it into Niagen IV and injectable formulations pursuant to a prescription [5].
- 503B facilities and compound pharmacies abroad distribute Niagen IV and injectable formulations [1].
- FDA-registered 503B facilities and compound pharmacies abroad use Niagen under applicable local requirements [7].
- Food-grade Niagen received NDI notifications in 2015 and 2018 [9] and GRAS notification in August 2016 [10].
- The 10-K lists tariffs and trade disputes as macro risk factors, not a business strategy [18].
- The company commits to meeting or exceeding FDA regulations [14].
- Operations are subject to regulation by the FDA, FTC, and other agencies [12].

## Sizing and survivability
A small fund needs about 98 trading days to build a position at $2.5M average daily dollar volume (reality check); net cash is $39.5M (reality check) and cash of $44.5M supports the current business (SEC XBRL). Share count is up 0.5% in 12 months and a shelf is on file (reality check), so dilution risk is low but present. Liquidity is adequate; thesis evidence is not.

## What would change our mind
A 10-K or 8-K disclosing named customer contracts, domestic capacity expansion, or capex tied to reshoring. A Form 4 with insider buying above $100K or multiple insiders. A federal award in USASpending to NAGE for domestic API production.
