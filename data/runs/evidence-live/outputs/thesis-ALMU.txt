# ALMU: The evidence does not support Aeluma as a pharma reshoring cleanroom supplier; it is an internal cleanroom operator with thin customer disclosure and insider selling.

## Bear case first
Why this name fails. The strongest disconfirming reading is that Aeluma does not sell cleanroom infrastructure to pharma facility builders. Its cleanroom is an R&D and manufacturing asset for its own use, not a product line. The filings mention no cleanroom equipment, no HVAC or filtration products, and no pharma facility contracts. The Form 4 shows one open-market sale and no buying, and the filings do not name any commercial customer. The pharma reshoring narrative is outside the disclosed business.

## Business today
The filings describe a 9,000 sq ft facility with an R&D and manufacturing cleanroom and rapid prototyping access [1][2]. The 8-K says Aeluma is developing a streamlined business model from its headquarters in Santa Barbara [0]; the 10-K says Aeluma is based in Goleta [2]. The excerpts do not identify the products sold or the end customers. Revenue was $4.7M and cash was $3.6M (SEC XBRL).

## Chain position
Aeluma does not occupy a demonstrated node in the pharmaceutical facility construction chain. A company that uses a cleanroom to make its own products is a customer of cleanroom suppliers, not one of those suppliers. The economic logic of pharma reshoring would require sales of controlled-environment infrastructure, and those sales are not disclosed.

## Customer graph
Customer evidence is thin. The identified buyers are government: Department of Defense awards of $2.05M and $1.00M and a NASA award of $0.85M, for $5.02M total (USASpending). No named enterprise customer appears in the excerpts (customer graph). Unproven: any commercial or pharma facility buyer. Six other SEC filers name Aeluma, which is third-party recognition but not proof of a direct customer relationship (customer graph).

## Insider and management signal
Trailing 12 months, Form 4 shows no open-market buys and one open-market sale with net dollar volume of -$2.83M (Form 4). Management language in the 8-K and 10-K is generic: a streamlined business model [0] and a cleanroom facility [1][2]. There are no quantified targets or committed capex.

## Street view
Sell-side aggregators show no available analyst consensus and no mean target (Street view). There are no firm ratings or price targets to evaluate. For an under-covered name, the absence of coverage removes an external validation channel.

## Non-linear case
The non-linear case would be federal awards scaling from the current $5.0M (USASpending) into recurring production contracts that use the R&D and manufacturing cleanroom described in [1][2] for high-value components. That case does not depend on pharmaceutical reshoring and would show up as named awards and revenue growth in filings. To get there, Aeluma would need to move from R&D and prototyping into repeatable production inside a 9,000 sq ft facility [1][2]. Without backlog or named customers, this remains a scenario rather than a finding.

## Evidence
- The 8-K says Aeluma is developing a streamlined business model from its Santa Barbara headquarters with a manufacturing cleanroom [0].
- The 10-K says Aeluma operates in a 9,000 sq ft facility with an R&D and manufacturing cleanroom and rapid prototyping access [1].
- The 10-K says Aeluma is based in Goleta, California, where it operates in a 9,000 sq ft facility with an R&D and manufacturing cleanroom and rapid prototyping access [2].

## Sizing and survivability
Average daily dollar volume is $14.9M, so building a position would take about 17 trading days (Reality check). Net cash is $3.6M and cash runway is about 12.6 quarters (SEC XBRL; Reality check). Share count increased 15.9% in the past 12 months and a shelf registration is on file, so dilution risk is live (Reality check). A small fund can build a position, but the thesis must overcome the equity overhang.

## What would change our mind
- A 10-K or 8-K that quantifies revenue from cleanroom products sold to pharma facility construction, not internal use.
- Form 4 open-market insider buys that reverse the trailing sale pattern.
- A new named federal award or customer disclosed in an 8-K, a 10-K, or USASpending.
