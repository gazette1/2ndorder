# LEU: Pharma reshoring thesis is unsupported by a uranium-only filing record

## Bear case first
The seed thesis fails on company-specific evidence. Revenue is $448.7M from uranium enrichment (SEC XBRL), and the filings describe nuclear fuel, not pharma ingredients [5][7][15]. Insider activity was net -$62.3K with 0 buys and 1 sale (Form 4). DOE awards total $147.95M with no named enterprise customers (USASpending). The direct disconfirming read is that a pharma policy wave does not reach a company with no pharma revenue or contracts.

## Business today
Centrus sells LEU and HALEU enrichment to utilities [7] and is the only NRC-licensed company actively enriching HALEU up to 20% U-235 [8][15]. Revenue was $448.7M, cash $1.96B (SEC XBRL).

## Chain position
Centrus is a fuel supplier in the nuclear adoption chain, not a process equipment maker. Its enriched uranium [5][7] is upstream of reactors, unrelated to pharma API plants. The economic logic depends on DOE contracts and advanced reactor demand.

## Customer graph
Government: DOE awards total $147.95M (USASpending). Enterprise: no named customers appear; utilities are a general customer class [7]. Unproven: no customer names beyond DOE. One other filer names the company (USASpending).

## Insider and management signal
Insider open-market activity was net -$62.3K, 0 buys and 1 sale (Form 4). Management emphasizes DOE agreements and centrifuge manufacturing [1][17], without pharma mention. The insider signal is a small negative.

## Street view
Coverage data are unavailable: unknown analysts, consensus n/a, mean target n/a. No firm rating actions are provided. Thin coverage is a setup feature, but it does not offset the business mismatch.

## Non-linear case
A non-linear outcome would require Centrus to enter pharma equipment or API manufacturing; no filing excerpt supports that. The visible non-linear path is a broader critical-supply-chain mandate funding domestic uranium enrichment [16][19]. That path needs new DOE contracts and is not the pharma thesis.

## Evidence
- Centrus is working with DOE on transition agreements, including a Piketon lease extension [1].
- Current enrichment involves HALEU and advanced nuclear fuel capabilities [5].
- Centrus supplies LEU to domestic and international utilities [7].
- AC100M is the only deployment-ready U.S. enrichment technology [8].
- Centrus is the only NRC-licensed company actively enriching HALEU to 20% U-235 [15].
- Ukraine war, Import Ban Act, and Russian Decree raised enrichment prices and prompted new capacity calls [16].
- Centrus began Piketon design work and domestic centrifuge manufacturing for commercial LEU [17].
- DOE continues to pursue HALEU availability and domestic enriched uranium for supply gaps [19].

## Sizing and survivability
At $137.3M average daily dollar volume, a small fund can build a position in about 2 trading days (Reality check). Net cash is $782.4M (Reality check), so funding is not a near-term issue. Share count is up 8% and a shelf is on file (Reality check), a dilution overhang; this does not rescue the thesis.

## What would change our mind
A 10-K or 8-K disclosing a pharma ingredient or process equipment segment would change the revenue view. A Form 4 open-market buy would change the insider signal. A federal award from HHS or FDA would add customer validation.
