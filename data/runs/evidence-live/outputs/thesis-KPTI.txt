# KPTI: Reshoring theme absent; cash burn and dilution make the equity a structural avoid

## Bear case first
The short fails if XPOVIO revenue stabilizes and the SENTRY trial data supports a supplemental approval that expands the label [0]. The company has an approved drug with $146.1M in trailing revenue (SEC XBRL), and the withdrawn DLBCL indication [2] is public, so that de-risking event may be priced in. Insiders have no open-market activity, so there is no selling pressure to confirm the short (Form 4). A distressed valuation with an approved oncology drug can attract acquirer interest, which caps downside.

## Business today
Karyopharm sells XPOVIO (selinexor), an approved cancer drug, and states it may never attain profitability or positive cash flows [3]. Trailing revenue is $146.1M and cash is $60.5M (SEC XBRL).

## Chain position
The theme node is US hospital and retail pharmacy generic drug supply risk, order 3, horizon long. Karyopharm does not occupy that node; it is an innovative biotech with a patented brand drug, no generic manufacturing, and no supply-chain exposure. There is no mechanism by which ingredient reshoring transfers to this revenue model.

## Customer graph
No federal award dollars and no named customers are disclosed (USASpending; filings); one other filer names the company (customer graph). The XPOVIO payer set is unproven from this evidence, with no enterprise or government buyer identified. Reimbursement coverage is a stated risk [18].

## Insider and management signal
Trailing 12-month open-market insider activity is zero: 0 buys, 0 sales, 0 insiders (Form 4). Management mentions tariffs only as a general risk with "limited" direct effects [5]. The provided filings contain no reshoring plans, commitments, or supply-chain capital allocation.

## Street view
Sell-side coverage is effectively absent: analyst count unknown, consensus not available, mean target not available. For an under-covered small-cap, the absence of coverage means there is no analyst floor supporting the equity.

## Non-linear case
The company agreed to withdraw the DLBCL accelerated approval [2] after an FDA meeting in March 2026 [1], shrinking the addressable label. Bispecific competitors TECVAYLI, ELREXFIO, and TALVEY were approved after XPOVIO [6], and a clinical setback could further compress perceptions of selinexor [8]. Net cash is -$108.8M and cash runway is about 3.2 quarters (reality check), so equity financing is likely; the 161.6% share count expansion and shelf registration (reality check) make dilutive issuance the base case. If the FDA requires additional data [19] or SENTRY sNDA timing slips [0], capital could be raised at a fraction of current levels.

## Evidence
- FDA meeting planned to discuss SENTRY data and the sNDA filing plan [0].
- FDA met in March 2026 regarding the DLBCL accelerated approval [1].
- FDA requested and Karyopharm agreed to voluntarily withdraw the DLBCL accelerated approval [2].
- Karyopharm may never attain profitability or positive cash flows [3].
- Tariffs and trade restrictions have had limited direct effects; macroeconomic pressures may contribute to instability [5].
- Bispecific competitors were approved after XPOVIO [6].
- Failure of a product candidate could negatively impact perceptions of selinexor [8].
- Reimbursement coverage may be limited and delayed [18].
- Regulatory authorities may require additional data, delaying or preventing approval [19].

## Sizing and survivability
A small fund can build a position in about 45 trading days at the $5.45M average daily dollar volume, but the setup is hostile: net cash is -$108.8M, cash runway is roughly 3.2 quarters, and the share count rose 161.6% with a shelf on file (reality check). Dilution is the likely funding path, which supports the short thesis but makes borrow and timing risk the dominant execution concerns.

## What would change our mind
- A Form 4 filing showing open-market insider buying would contradict the zero-conviction insider signal (Form 4).
- A 10-Q or 8-K disclosing a named customer, federal award, or supply-chain investment would connect Karyopharm to the reshoring theme.
- FDA approval or a public SENTRY readout supporting the sNDA [0] would change the dilution and withdrawal probabilities.
