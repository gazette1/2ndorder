# AQST: Reshoring theme does not apply; the actual short is an Anaphylm FDA binary with 2.4 quarters of cash runway

## Bear case first

The reshoring short fails because the evidence does not connect AQST to API manufacturing or import exposure; all six excerpts concern Anaphylm FDA approval timing and loan terms [0]-[5]. No excerpt mentions tariffs, foreign suppliers, or API production. The company has $44.545M revenue and no insider selling (Form 4), so the disconfirming read of the dossier is no insider distress signal and no customer concentration to attack. The reshoring short is a thematic mismatch.

## Business today

AQST has $44.545M revenue and $31.807M cash (SEC XBRL). It has filed a New Drug Application for Anaphylm, and its credit agreement ties Tranche B loan availability to FDA approval before June 30, 2027 [0]. The excerpts do not disclose product revenue breakdowns or customer names.

## Non-linear case

The compounding downside path runs through Anaphylm approval failure. If approval is not received by June 30, 2027, Tranche B cannot be borrowed [0]. If approval is not received by December 31, 2027, lenders may require a partial loan paydown [5], and the loan amount is reduced dollar-for-dollar by mandatory prepayments [1]. With cash runway of about 2.4 quarters and net cash of $-13.193M (reality check), a paydown demand forces equity issuance under the shelf registration, and share count growth accelerates from the current 25.9% 12-month pace (reality check). The financing spiral is the non-linear outcome.

- Form 4 filings showing open-market insider buying, signaling conviction that approval is on track.
