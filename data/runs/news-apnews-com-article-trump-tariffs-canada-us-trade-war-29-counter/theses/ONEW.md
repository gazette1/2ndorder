# ONEW: Tariff resolution lowers floor plan costs, but filings show no new cross-border financing business

## Bear case first
The filings describe tariffs as an uncertain risk, not an opportunity [0][1][13][14]. The floor plan facility is an existing working capital tool, not a new cross-border segment [2][3][18]. The filings show no dated catalyst, design win, or named program tied to the theme. Lower financing costs would not create new revenue. The company is a net debtor at -$269.4M (reality check), customer evidence is thin, and the only positive is one insider's buying (Form 4).

## Business today
OneWater sells new and pre-owned boats and parts to retail consumers, funding inventory through a Wells Fargo floor plan [2][3]. Revenue was $1.87B, cash $64.8M at September 30, 2025 (SEC XBRL).

## Chain position
Second-order beneficiary. ONEW borrows in the inventory financing chain; it does not lend. Tariff resolution would lower financing and input costs, but the filings show no new business line.
