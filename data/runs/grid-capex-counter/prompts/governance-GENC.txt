Recovery of Executive Compensation We have adopted a Clawback Policy which permits us to recover all or a portion of any performance-based compensation that was received by a covered executive on or after October 2, 2023, including equity awards, if our financial statements are restated as a result of errors, omissions, or fraud. The amount which may be recovered will be the amount by which the affected compensation exceeded the amount that would have been payable had the financial statements been initially filed as restated.
