# MEI: Flat US grid capex pushes this electrical equipment supplier toward data center power infrastructure, but the pivot is currently unproven.

## Bear case first
The disconfirming reading is that the data center power infrastructure statement in the 8-K is a general participation claim, not a revenue or order fact [0]. The 10-K sales bridge attributes growth to power distribution products and off-highway lighting, not to data centers [4]. The dossier shows no insider open-market buying (Form 4), no federal awards (USASpending), and no named customers, so the thesis rests on capacity-shift logic rather than visible demand. If utility T&D orders flatten before data center contracts appear, MEI has no offsetting disclosed order flow.

## Business today
Methode sells power distribution products, including busbars, smart connect systems, battery disconnect units, and integrated circuit boards, plus user interfaces, LED lighting, and sensors [1][2]. The 10-K describes these capabilities as a full spectrum of vehicle systems [3], and the sales growth discussion points to commercial vehicle and off-highway markets [4]. As of 2026-05-02, revenue was $1,019.2M and cash was $139.6M (SEC XBRL).

## Chain position
MEI occupies the second-order beneficiary node in the data center buildout: a diversified electrical equipment supplier that can redirect existing switchgear, busway, and transformer capacity to private data center contracts. The economic logic is capacity utilization. Factories need volume to cover fixed costs, so flat utility T&D orders push suppliers toward the faster-growing data center market even at more competitive prices.

## Customer graph
No named enterprise customers appear in the excerpts (unproven). Federal awards are $0 on two Department of Defense contract numbers (USASpending). Three other SEC filers name MEI (SEC XBRL), but the excerpts do not identify them as buyers. Customer evidence is thin and does not validate the data center thesis.

## Insider and management signal
Form 4 records zero open-market insider buys and zero sales in the trailing 12 months (Form 4). Management language is positive, saying MEI has a more focused portfolio, deeper customer relationships, and growing participation in markets including data center power infrastructure [0]. The language is not backed by a named program, committed capex, or a dated milestone.

## Street view
Sell-side aggregators show an unknown analyst count, no consensus, and no mean target (Street view). No firm rating actions can be named from the available data. For a small-cap with no visible sell-side coverage, the absence of coverage is part of the setup.

## Non-linear case
The path to a multiple of today's business is a re-rating from an auto and industrial supplier to a data center power supplier. If MEI converts existing busbar, switchgear, and battery disconnect products into data center electrical room orders, revenue growth could come with limited incremental capital. What has to be true is that utility T&D capex stays flat, data center developers buy private electrical equipment contracts, and MEI wins enough volume to cover fixed costs. The filings do not yet show those design wins or customer commitments.

## Evidence
- The 8-K states a belief that Methode enters the year with a more focused portfolio, a stronger operating model, deeper customer relationships, and growing participation in industrial growth markets, including data center power infrastructure [0].
- The 8-K lists capabilities including power distribution, busbars, smart connect systems, battery disconnect units, integrated circuit boards, user interface components, LED lighting, and sensors [1].
- The 10-K contains the same capability language [2].
- The 10-K says these capabilities form a full spectrum of vehicle systems, from power distribution to user interface components, LED lighting, and sensor applications [3].
- The 10-K attributes an increase to higher sales volumes for power distribution products and higher off-highway lighting volumes, partially offset by lower commercial vehicle lighting volumes [4].

## Sizing and survivability
A small fund can build a position in about 23 trading days at the stated average daily dollar volume of $11.1M (reality check). Net cash is -$185.4M, so debt exceeds cash despite the $139.6M cash balance (reality check). Share count fell 0.6% in the trailing 12 months, so dilution is not a current threat (reality check).

## What would change our mind
An 8-K disclosing a named data center customer, design win, or order backlog would validate the pivot. A 10-K segment note quantifying data center revenue, backlog, or capacity additions would show materiality. A Form 4 open-market purchase by an insider would add conviction where none exists today.
