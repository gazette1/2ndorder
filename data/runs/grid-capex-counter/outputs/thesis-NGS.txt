# NGS: Compressor aftermarket services show no evidence of exposure to the data center gas turbine theme

## Bear case first
The disconfirming reading of the evidence is direct: NGS describes aftermarket services for customer-owned compressor equipment, not gas turbines [0][4]. Aftermarket services were an insignificant portion of overall gross margins in 2025 and 2024 [6], and compressor sales and aftermarket services margins tend to be substantially lower than rental margins [2]. Insider open-market selling was net negative at $7.779M over the trailing 12 months (Form 4). The filings show no named data center customers, no OEM programs, and no federal awards (USASpending), so the gas turbine aftermarket thesis has no filing support.

## Business today
NGS reports three operating units: Rental, Sales, and Aftermarket Services [1]. Rental service agreements include a fee for servicing the compressor unit and surcharges for fluids [3]; aftermarket services are routine or call-out services on customer-owned equipment and commissioning of new units [4], with primary costs in labor, support costs, materials and supplies [5]. Revenue was $15.008M and cash was $0 as of 2023-12-31 (SEC XBRL).

## Chain position
The relevant adoption chain node is gas turbine aftermarket service provider. NGS's filings place it in compressor aftermarket services instead [0][4]. The recurring-revenue logic of turbine parts, overhauls, and monitoring does not apply to the documented business.

## Customer graph
No named enterprise customers appear in the excerpts, and federal award dollars are $0 (USASpending). One other filer names NGS, but that is not evidence of a paying customer. The buyer base is unproven from the available evidence.

## Insider and management signal
Form 4 filings over the trailing 12 months show net open-market selling of $7.779M, with 3 buys and 23 sales by one insider (Form 4). Management lists aftermarket services as an operating unit [1] but provides no named programs, committed capex, or quantified targets, and says the unit was insignificant to overall gross margins [6]. The language does not express conviction in a gas turbine aftermarket opportunity.

## Street view
Sell-side aggregators report no analyst count, consensus, or mean target for NGS. No recent firm rating actions are reported. Thin coverage is part of the setup, but it also means no outside validation of the thesis.

## Non-linear case
The non-linear case would require NGS to become a gas turbine aftermarket service provider for data center on-site generation. That would require new OEM partnerships, named data center customers, and aftermarket revenue that moves from insignificant [6] to material. The filings show no such contracts, capex, or business line. Without those, any multiple on today's revenue is unsupported speculation.

## Sizing and survivability
Average daily dollar volume is $5.386M, so about 46 trading days to build a position (reality check). Net cash is $0 and cash is $0 (reality check; SEC XBRL), so no funded runway is visible. Share count rose 2.9 percent in 12 months, adding dilution risk to an already weak equity story.

## What would change our mind
1. A 10-K or 10-Q that reports aftermarket services as a material gross margin contributor or names gas turbine aftermarket contracts.
2. A Form 4 showing net open-market insider buying rather than net selling.
3. A federal award to NGS for gas turbine or compressor aftermarket services (USASpending).
