## Non-linear case
The downside path compounds if standby and demand charges are approved in jurisdictions with large data center load, which raises the fixed cost of behind-the-meter generation. If FVR owns real estate used by data centers or self-generating tenants, those tenants could face higher occupancy costs, weaker rent collections, and covenant stress. The 10-K covenants include a 60% total leverage ratio and a 1.50x fixed charge coverage minimum [3]; a rent shortfall would bring those into focus. The non-linear step is a dividend cut or equity issuance through the shelf, which would push the stock lower than a simple earnings decline. This path requires FVR to have an actual data center or energy-linked tenant base, which the filings do not demonstrate.
