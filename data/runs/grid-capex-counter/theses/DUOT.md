# DUOT: On-site gas turbine fleets turn data center load growth into asset management fees outside utility capex

## Bear case first
The strongest disconfirming reading is that the Fortress program is already shrinking. New APR began scaling back the scope of services under the AMA [9], and the excerpts show no dated milestones such as design wins. Customer concentration is effectively one Fortress-related complex, New APR and Sawgrass. Insider open-market activity is zero (Form 4), and cash runway of about 0.6 quarters (reality check) means the company cannot outlast a slow ramp. No segment revenue breakdown is disclosed [6].

## Business today
DUOT sells asset management, sales, and operations services for mobile gas turbine fleets. Under the AMA, Duos Energy manages deployment and operations of a fleet of mobile gas turbines and balance-of-plant inventory [1][2]. Revenue is $27.0MM and cash is $1.9MM (SEC XBRL).

## Chain position
DUOT is the asset manager for behind-the-meter mobile turbine fleets, downstream of manufacturers but directly exposed to fleet deployment. It earns fees for managing capacity that bypasses utility interconnection queues, so revenue is levered to data center load growth, not utility T&D spending.

## Customer graph
Enterprise buyers: Fortress Investment Group, New APR, and Sawgrass APR Holdings LLC (customer graph). These three are one transaction complex, so the customer graph is effectively one enterprise relationship. Government buyer: $2.6MM in federal awards from the Department of Transportation (USASpending). One other filer names the company (customer graph). Customer evidence is concentrated, not broad.

## Insider and management signal

Management language is specific and committed: it names Fortress, New APR, and Sawgrass, and quantifies approximately 850 MW and 30 turbine generators [0][7]. The disclosure that New APR is scaling back the AMA scope [9] tempers that commitment.

## Street view

## Non-linear case
The 850 MW under management [0] compares against $27.0MM revenue (SEC XBRL), so a small management fee per megawatt could be material relative to current revenue. If Fortress re-expands the AMA scope, or if DUOT signs similar asset management contracts with other turbine fleet owners, the fee stream could multiply. This requires data center operators to keep choosing on-site gas turbines over grid interconnection and DUOT to win repeat asset management contracts.

## Evidence
- In late 2024, the Company engaged with Fortress to assist in FIG's purchase of approximately 850 MW of electrical generation capacity, 30 mobile gas turbine generators [0][7].
- Under the AMA, Duos Energy manages the deployment and operations for a fleet of mobile gas turbines and balance-of-plant inventory [1][2].
- In December 2024, the Company entered into contracts with Fortress, and a $5.0MM cash advance was recorded as of December 31, 2024 [4].
- The Asset Management Services segment generates revenues through the AMA with New APR [6].
- New APR began scaling back the scope of services provided under the AMA [9].

## Sizing and survivability
Liquidity supports a small fund position: $10.5MM average daily dollar volume, about 24 trading days to build (reality check). Net cash is $1.9MM with cash runway of about 0.6 quarters (reality check), which threatens the thesis. Share count is up 62.1 percent in 12 months and a shelf registration is on file (reality check), so dilution risk is high.

## What would change our mind
- An 8-K or 10-Q disclosing a new or expanded asset management contract with a counterparty outside the Fortress complex.
- Form 4 filings showing insider open-market purchases.
- A USASpending federal award to DUOT for mobile turbine deployment, which would validate government demand.
