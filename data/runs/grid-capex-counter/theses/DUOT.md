# DUOT: Managing mobile gas turbines for behind-the-meter power as utility T&D capex flatlines

## Bear case first
DUOT's visible revenue program is an AMA with New APR, and New APR is scaling back scope [9]. The 850 MW is purchased capacity, not a guaranteed fee base; utilization, fee rates, and contract duration are undisclosed [0]. Customers are concentrated in one Fortress-linked program [0][4][5]. Insiders have not bought stock (Form 4), and cash runway is about 0.6 quarters (Reality check). If data center demand grows, DUOT must convert it into expanded AMA scope or new named customers; the filing does not show that.

## Business today
DUOT's Asset Management Services segment generates revenue under an AMA with New APR to manage mobile gas turbines and balance-of-plant inventory [6]. Total revenue was $27.0M and cash was $1.9M at 2025-12-31 (SEC XBRL). Segment revenue is not broken out in the excerpts.

## Chain position
DUOT is not an aeroderivative manufacturer, the first-order beneficiary in the seed map. It sits downstream, managing 850 MW of mobile gas turbines [0]. Fees scale with deployment and operation of behind-the-meter turbines, not with utility T&D capex.

## Customer graph
Named enterprise customers are Fortress, New APR, and Sawgrass [0][4][5]. The filing refers to the counterparty as New APR in [4] and Sawgrass in [5]. Government buyer is Department of Transportation, $2.56M (USASpending); one other filer names DUOT (Customer graph). No data center operator is a named customer, so the data-center link is unproven.

## Insider and management signal
Form 4 shows no open-market insider buying or selling in the trailing 12 months (Form 4). Management names specific counterparties and capacity [0][1], but discloses that New APR is scaling back the AMA [9], so the signal is mixed.

## Street view
No sell-side coverage exists: analyst count unknown, consensus n/a, mean target n/a (Street view). No rating actions are present. Thin coverage is part of the setup.

## Non-linear case
The non-linear path: data center operators need firm power faster than interconnection queues, and Fortress/New APR add more turbines for DUOT to manage. If fees track capacity or utilization, revenue can grow without utility capex. The filing shows a reduction, not an expansion [9], so the path needs a reversal visible in a future 10-Q. Fee structure is undisclosed, so sensitivity is an open question.

## Evidence
- DUOT assisted FIG in purchasing approximately 850 MW, consisting of 30 mobile gas turbine generators and balance-of-plant equipment [0].
- Under the AMA, Duos Energy manages the fleet for New APR [1].
- In December 2024, DUOT entered a series of contracts with Fortress, and a $5.0M cash advance was recognized as revenue in 2025 [4][5].
- The Asset Management Services segment generates revenues through the AMA with New APR [6].
- New APR began to scale back the scope of services under the AMA [9].
