# SEI: No disclosed link to the behind-the-meter project finance thesis, despite flat grid capex

## Bear case first
A disconfirming reading is that SEI is an energy infrastructure holding company with no disclosed exposure to the thesis. The 10-K excerpt defines the company but does not describe a segment, product, or customer [3]. A director's outside board seat at MPLX, a midstream MLP [2], points toward conventional logistics, not distributed generation. One insider sold $155.8M net over trailing 12 months (Form 4), no customer is named, and federal awards are $0 (USASpending). The thesis therefore rests on an assumed role that the filings do not support.

## Non-linear case
If US grid capital expenditure flatlines and data centers demand on-site power, project finance for behind-the-meter gas turbines and solar-plus-storage could compound. SEI could become a lender or lessor to those developers, and each bankable project would lower the next developer's cost of capital. That outcome would be a multiple of today's business if SEI's disclosed revenue shifted to project finance fees and lease income. For that to happen, the filings must show a new segment, a project pipeline, or named counterparties. None of that appears in the excerpts [3].
