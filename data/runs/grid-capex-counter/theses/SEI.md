## Chain position
The seed thesis is that US grid capex stays near $180B annually through 2030, so data center load growth is met by behind-the-meter gas turbines and solar plus storage. The map puts SEI at order 2, a beneficiary of project finance for that buildout as capital reallocates from flat utility rate base to independent power producers, gas turbine lessors, and distributed generation platforms. No filing evidence places SEI in that node.

None of that is visible in the excerpts [0][1][2][3].
