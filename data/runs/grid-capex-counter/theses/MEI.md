The mechanism is capacity utilization: flat utility T&D orders push factories to chase private data center contracts with similar content, and a broad product line offsets lost volume, though pricing is more competitive. ## Non-linear case

Data center power becomes material if Methode converts busbar, BDU, and power distribution capability [1] into private contract wins. The non-linear path requires flat utility grid capex to force a lasting capacity reallocation, not a temporary order surge. If data center content per megawatt is large and Methode captures share, revenue shifts from cyclical vehicle markets to contracted projects, and the multiple re-rates. Required: a named design win, disclosed revenue, or capacity commitment.
