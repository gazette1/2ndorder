# NWE: Flat T&D capex stalls rate base growth and turns the 2027 equity issuance into a dilution event.

## Bear case first
Flat T&D capex removes the rate base growth that allowed revenue depends on [8]; the name fails if that flat capex path is real. The disconfirming read is that management affirmed 2026 EPS guidance, targets 4-6% rate base growth, and named a merger partner [1][3], all of which could protect the stock and make the short fail. The filings do not show signed data center load commitments, so the growth plan is an open question. Insider selling is zero (Form 4), but customer evidence outside a small federal award is thin (USASpending).

## Business today
NWE sells regulated electricity and gas service in Montana and South Dakota; the Montana gas revenue requirement includes a step-down from gas production depletion [6], and South Dakota coal generation revenue is not easily identifiable due to bundled rates [7]. NWE is a fully regulated utility where rate base is the primary earnings driver [8]. Revenue was $414.265MM and cash was $8.781MM (SEC XBRL).

## Chain position
NWE occupies the rate-regulated electric utility node, order 1, at-risk, mid horizon. Rate base is the primary earnings driver [8]; the 2024 estimated rate base was $5.38 billion [0]. The company targets 4-6% rate base growth [1], and under flat T&D capex that is the vulnerable assumption.

The targets are quantified, but no customer contracts support them.

## Street view
Sell-side aggregators show no analyst count, no consensus, and no target (Street view). No firm rating actions are available. The absence of a named rating leaves the stock under-covered, which means the short thesis is not being argued by visible sell-side voices.

## Non-linear case
The downside path starts if national T&D capex flatlines near $180 billion a year and data center load is served behind the meter. NWE then cannot add rate base for that load, and the 2027 equity issuance [2] lands when growth is stalling. If the Black Hills merger [3] closes on terms that dilute holders, or fails, EPS growth falls below the 4-6% guidance [1]. Coal generation provides only 7-8% of earnings [9], so coal performance cannot offset the shortfall. Each year of flat capex removes the next year's rate base growth, so the earnings gap compounds from a one-time miss into a multi-year shortfall.

## What would change our mind
An 8-K reporting a state commission order that approves a rate base increase above the flat national trend would disconfirm the mechanism. A Form 4 showing open-market insider purchases would signal internal conviction. A 10-Q disclosure naming a data center as a counterparty would replace the unproven load assumption.
