## Chain position
The company is an order-3 beneficiary with a long horizon. Utilities earn standby and connection fees from self-generating data centers, which makes them more willing to host behind-the-meter generation. Hyliion benefits because data centers need generators like KARNO to self-generate while retaining grid connection, and its placement near load avoids T&D losses [1].
