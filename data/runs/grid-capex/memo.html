Trigger

Actor: US regulated utilities and transmission owners, with data center developers as anchor customers. Action: commit to a multi-year acceleration of grid transmission, distribution, and substation capital expenditures. Magnitude: annual US grid capex rises by roughly $40 billion to $60 billion above the 2025 baseline by 2030, supporting an incremental 30 to 50 GW of data center load. Geography: United States, concentrated in PJM, ERCOT, Southeast, and Southwest markets. Timing: 2026 to 2030, with orders and rate cases starting in 2025 and construction peaking in 2028 to 2030. Certainty: assumed; several utility plans are announced but depend on signed data center load contracts and regulatory approval. Reversibility: utilities can defer or cancel project tranches if data center load fails to materialize or regulators disallow cost recovery.
