# POWI: Grid capex supercycle exposure exists, but evidence is unquantified and insiders are selling

## Bear case first
The grid capex theme is an existing end market, not a new option. The filings describe products already sold into HVDC transmission, renewables, and data centers, with no grid-specific market sizing or revenue split. Insider selling is heavy: net -$30.3MM over 12 months with 0 buys (Form 4). Customer evidence is thin: no named customers and no federal awards (USASpending). The filings contain no near-term catalysts such as design wins or qualification milestones.

## Business today
POWI sells system-level power-conversion semiconductors that integrate a high-voltage transistor, drivers, control circuitry, and optional isolated communication links [3]. Applications include solar and wind power, HVDC transmission, industrial motors, electric locomotives, EVs, and AI data center power supplies [1][2][4][5]. Revenue is $443.5MM and cash is $58.8MM (SEC XBRL).

## Chain position
The map position names specialty high-voltage electrical contractors as the beneficiary, but the filings describe a semiconductor supplier, not a contractor. POWI sits upstream as a component maker whose products go into high-power conversion equipment used in grid and data center builds. The economic logic is that grid capex drives demand for power semiconductors, but the filings do not quantify the link.

## Customer graph
Buyers are unproven from the evidence: no named enterprise customers, $0 in federal awards (USASpending), and no agencies identified. Three other SEC filers name POWI, but the excerpts do not establish whether they are customers, partners, or suppliers. The customer graph is thin.

## Insider and management signal
Form 4 data records 36 open-market sales and 0 buys over the trailing 12 months, net -$30.3MM, with the insider filer count listed as 0 (Form 4). Management language is descriptive: demand drivers are "compelling" [0], products are "key building blocks" [1] and "critical components" [5], with no quantified targets or committed capital. The insider signal is negative.

## Street view
Sell-side aggregator data shows no analyst count, no consensus, and no mean target, indicating coverage is thin or absent. No firm ratings or actions are reported. For a small-cap with $71.8MM average daily dollar volume, thin coverage is part of the setup, but also means no external validation of the thesis.

## Non-linear case
The non-linear outcome requires grid and data center capex to translate into a step-change in high-power semiconductor content. POWI's products span 100 kW to gigawatts [2][6], which covers HVDC transmission and large-scale renewable connections. If AI data center load accelerates HVDC buildout, POWI's gate drivers and switches could see order growth beyond the current revenue base. That path requires the company to convert descriptive demand into named programs and revenue growth visible in filings; the current evidence does not show it.

## Evidence
- Demand drivers from renewable energy, grid infrastructure, and AI data centers are described as compelling [0].
- Products are "key building blocks" in clean power, from milliwatts to megawatts, in AI data centers, EVs, and HVDC infrastructure [1].
- Switches and drivers serve 100 kW to gigawatt applications: industrial motors, solar and wind, electric locomotives, EVs, and HVDC transmission [2].
- Products are system-level, integrating a high-voltage transistor, drivers, control circuitry, and optional isolated communication [3].
- Expected applications include AI data center power supplies, communications infrastructure, and EV onboard charging [4].
- Gate-driver products are "critical components" in DC motor drives, solar and wind, HVDC transmission, and electric locomotives [5].
- The same high-power application list as [2] appears again [6].

## Sizing and survivability
A small fund can build a position in about 1 trading day, given $71.8MM average daily dollar volume (reality check). Net cash is $58.8MM with a $443.5MM revenue base, and share count declined 0.7% over 12 months, so no dilution risk and some buyback (SEC XBRL). Liquidity and survivability do not threaten the thesis.

## What would change our mind
- A Form 4 filing showing insider open-market buys, or a halt in the 36-sale selling pattern.
- A 10-Q or 8-K announcing a named design win, customer program, or grid-specific revenue target.
- A federal award in USASpending or a named customer in a filing that identifies who pays POWI for grid-related products.
