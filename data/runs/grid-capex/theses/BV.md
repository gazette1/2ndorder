# BV: Recurring landscape maintenance with no filed exposure to the gas turbine aftermarket supercycle

## Bear case first
The filing describes BrightView exclusively as landscape maintenance and development services. Revenue in [7] is entirely from Landscape Maintenance, Snow Removal, and Development Services. The excerpts contain no mention of gas turbines, engines, aftermarket parts, or overhaul services. The data center load supercycle creates no new business for this company on the filing evidence. An investor buying this thesis would be buying a seasonal landscaping contractor, not an energy aftermarket provider.

## Business today
BrightView sells recurring landscape maintenance, snow removal, and short-duration enhancement services under contracts that run one to three years and are cancellable with 30-90 days notice [3][4][13]. Customers include Fortune 500 campuses, HOAs, public parks, hotels, airports, municipalities, hospitals, schools, restaurants, and golf courses [16]. Net service revenues were $717.6MM for the quarter and $2,035.3MM for the nine months ended June 30, 2026 [7]; revenue was not reported as of 2025-09-30 and cash was $74.5MM (SEC XBRL).

## Chain position
BrightView occupies no node in a gas turbine or engine service and aftermarket adoption chain. Its route-based maintenance operations [15] have no economic link to data center load, run hours, or maintenance intervals. The map position is unsupported by the evidence.

## Customer graph

## Insider and management signal
Trailing 12 months show net open-market insider activity of $0 across 0 buys and 0 sales by 0 insiders (Form 4). Management language focuses on recurring contracts and segment revenue [2][11], with no discussion of data center load or energy infrastructure.

## Street view

## Non-linear case
A path to a multiple of today's business would require BrightView to enter or be acquired into gas turbine aftermarket services, or to win data center campus landscape contracts at a scale that changes the revenue mix. Neither path is visible in the filing, which shows no energy-related services [1][2][13]. The non-linear case rests on unstated diversification or M&A not evidenced in the excerpts.

## Evidence

- Maintenance Services revenue comes primarily from landscape maintenance and snow removal [2].
- Landscape maintenance contracts are recurring, one to three years, cancellable with 30-90 days notice [3].
- Enhancement services are short-duration supplemental work [4].
- Net service revenues were $717.6MM and $2,035.3MM for the three and nine months ended June 30, 2026 [7].
- Maintenance Services delivers recurring landscaping, snow removal, water management, irrigation, tree care, and golf course maintenance [11][13].
- Maintenance services are route-based through a national branch network [15].
- Customers include corporate campuses, HOAs, public parks, hotels, airports, municipalities, hospitals, schools, restaurants, and golf courses [16].

## Sizing and survivability
Average daily dollar volume is $12.4MM, so a small fund needs roughly 20 trading days to build a position (reality check). Net cash is -$715.7MM (SEC XBRL), and cash of $74.5MM is thin against that debt. The share count is down 2.4% over 12 months, showing buybacks, but the balance sheet is the binding constraint.

## What would change our mind
- A 10-Q or 10-K disclosing gas turbine, engine, or aftermarket service revenue in the segment or revenue note.
- Form 4 open-market insider buying, signaling conviction in a new direction.
- A federal award tied to data center or energy infrastructure in USASpending, connecting customers to the theme.
