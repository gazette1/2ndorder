# T1 Energy: Avoid solar module supplier with unverified backlog and delay risk.

## Bear case first
T1's stated 5.4 GW of customer contracts [7] is not a firm revenue commitment; contracts can be amended to decrease volume or change delivery schedules [9]. The provided filing excerpts do not name any customer, and federal award data shows no government demand (USASpending). Insider open-market sales totaled $1.9MM against no buys (Form 4). Production is concentrated in a single facility [5], and inventory rose from $116.0MM to $228.8MM in six months [10]. A renewable project delay cycle would push module deliveries later, compress pricing, and expose this concentration.

## Business today
T1 manufactures and sells PV solar modules in the United States, with one operating segment [1][11]. Modules are produced at G1_Dallas, which became fully operational in Q2 2025 [17]. 2025 revenue was $755.295MM and cash was $182.450MM (SEC XBRL).

## Chain position
T1 sits at the equipment supplier node of the US grid capex adoption chain. The data center load story creates module demand, but suppliers face order pushouts and pricing pressure when renewable projects slip. For an at-risk node, the company is a short or avoid candidate, not a long.

## Customer graph
The provided filing excerpts report customer contracts for 5.4 GW [7] but do not identify the buyers. Federal awards are $0 (USASpending), and no other filer names the company (customer graph). Enterprise customers are unproven; government customers are absent.

## Insider and management signal
Trailing 12-month Form 4 activity is net selling of $1,871,777 across four open-market sales and zero buys (Form 4). Management gives quantified delivery schedules [8], a 5.4 GW contracted volume [7], and a Q1 2027 cell production start [3]. The operational specificity does not offset the absence of insider buying.

## Street view
Sell-side coverage is absent: analyst count unknown, consensus n/a, mean target n/a (Street view). No firm rating actions are visible. The stock is under-covered, which removes a support channel when the bear thesis is active.

## Non-linear case
The downside scenario is a delay-oversupply spiral. Customers use amendment rights [9] to push 2026 and 2027 deliveries [8] into later years or cancel. T1 remains at a single facility [5] with rising finished goods inventory [10]. Net cash is -$207.708MM and share count is up 88.9 percent with a shelf on file (Reality check), so an equity raise is a likely part of the path. The result is a share price far below today's level.

## Evidence
- T1 manufactures and sells PV solar modules in the United States [1].
- A facility is anticipated to begin production of TOPCon solar cells in Q1 2027 [3].
- PV solar modules are currently produced at a single facility in the United States [5].
- Contracts with customers for future sale of 5.4 GW of PV solar modules exist as of June 30, 2026 [7].
- T1 expects to recognize sales of 1.5 GW in the remainder of 2026, 1.3 GW in 2027, and 2.6 GW thereafter through 2029 [8].
- These contracts may be amended to increase or decrease volume or change delivery schedules [9].
- Inventory at June 30, 2026 was $228.773MM total, including $225.562MM of raw materials and $2.024MM of finished goods, versus $116.043MM total at December 31, 2025 [10].
- The single operating segment derives revenue from the manufacturing and sale of PV solar modules [11].
- G1_Dallas became fully operational in Q2 2025 and production increased through 2025 [17].

## Sizing and survivability
Average daily dollar volume is $280.874MM, so a small fund can build a position in about one trading day (Reality check). Net cash is -$207.708MM, share count rose 88.9 percent in 12 months, and a shelf registration is on file (Reality check). The liquidity is adequate, but the negative cash position, dilution history, and shelf do not support a long thesis.

## What would change our mind
A 10-Q or 10-K naming a contracted customer would improve the unproven customer evidence. A Form 4 showing an insider open-market buy would change the insider signal. A USASpending federal award to T1 would add a government buyer.
