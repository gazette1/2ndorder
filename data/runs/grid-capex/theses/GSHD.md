# GSHD: Insurance franchise with no rate-case exposure; short lacks a mechanism

## Bear case first
Why the short fails: no utility rate base, no prudence review, and no grid capex exposure appear in the filing excerpts. GSHD sells insurance franchises; revenue definitions describe franchise fees and interest income only [1][2][10]. A short built on regulatory disallowances of utility projects has no transmission channel to GSHD earnings. The disconfirming read of the dossier: insider selling of $3.54MM (Form 4) is modest against the $1,544MM market cap, and the absence of named customers means no single buyer can disrupt revenue. The composite score measures thematic absence, not operational deterioration.

## Business today
GSHD sells insurance franchises to agents, collecting royalties, initial franchise fees, and interest income on franchise payment plans [2][5][6]. Revenue is split into Core Revenue, Cost Recovery Revenue, and Ancillary Revenue, all non-GAAP measures [3]. Cost Recovery Revenue was $1.5MM in Q2 2026 and $3.2MM in H1 2026 [12][13]. SEC XBRL reports $0 revenue and $34.39MM cash at 2024-12-31, which conflicts with the 10-Q revenue categories; the excerpts do not resolve the conflict.

## Non-linear case
The theme-based non-linear case does not exist: the company has no rate base and no prudence review exposure. The downside path that does compound is a compression of the franchise finance model. Initial franchise fees and interest income are cost recovery streams tied to selling and financing franchise units [5][6]. If franchisee recruitment slows, both streams decline, and the non-GAAP adjusted metrics management emphasizes fall in parallel [7][8]. The path compounds if payment plan defaults force write-downs of the receivables behind interest income.
