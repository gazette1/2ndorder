## Business today

## Chain position

SolarMax is the EPC contractor node in the US solar and storage adoption chain. It performs construction services for large-scale BESS projects [6][8]. The economic logic is that EPC firms capture a meaningful share of project spend through fixed-price and cost-plus contracts, and multi-year construction timelines create backlog visibility.

## Non-linear case

The three Dec 31, 2025 BESS contracts [8][14], if executed, can grow revenue from the current $60.2M EPC base [7] against a projected $125B US solar market [4]. The company has prior EPC experience from Chinese solar farms [9] and states it is expanding US commercial and industrial services [3]. For the non-linear outcome, new customers must appear beyond the four named entities, and future 10-Q and 10-K filings must show revenue from the Puerto Rico and Corpus Christi projects. The path to a multiple of today's business depends on customer diversification and execution margins.
