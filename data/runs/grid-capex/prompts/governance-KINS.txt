PROPOSAL 1: ELECTION OF DIRECTORS Six directors are to be elected at the meeting to serve until the next annual meeting of stockholders and until their respective successors shall have been elected and have qualified. Our Restated Certificate of Incorporation provides for cumulative voting of shares for the election of directors. This means that each stockholder has the right to cumulate his or her votes and give to one or more nominees as many votes as equals the number of directors to be elected (six) multiplied by the number of shares he is entitled to vote. A stockholder may therefore cast his or her votes for one nominee or distribute them among two or more of the nominees. 19 Nominees for Director All of the nominees are currently members of our Board of Directors.
