Why did I receive a Notice in the mail regarding the availability of proxy materials on the internet? We are pleased to take advantage of rules of the Securities and Exchange Commission (the SEC ) that allow companies to furnish their proxy materials over the internet. Accordingly, we are sending to most of our stockholders of record a Notice of Internet Availability of Proxy Materials (the Notice ) on or about April 22, 2026. All stockholders will have the ability to access the proxy materials on the website referred to in the Notice or request to receive a printed set of the proxy materials. Instructions on how to access the proxy materials over the internet or to request a printed copy may be found in the Notice.
