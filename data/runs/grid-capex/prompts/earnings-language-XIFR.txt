We also continue to make meaningful progress on our capital plan. We have now completed approximately 50% of our planned 2026 repowerings. We also advanced our previously announced battery storage co-investment agreement with NextEra Energy Resources by forming the Mammoth Plains Energy Storage and Carousel Energy Storage joint ventures and by completing the associated sales of interconnection assets and rights in July. XPLR Infrastructure also reported first-quarter 2026 adjusted EBITDA of $435 million, and free cash flow before growth (FCFBG) for the first quarter of 2026 was $89 million. The year-over-year decline in FCFBG was consistent with the company's expectations as it was primarily driven by higher financing costs resulting from balance sheet simplification and capital plan funding activities in 2025. Specifically, XPLR Infrastructure s first-quarter 2026 FCFBG includes approximately $74 million of incremental corporate interest expense from the approximately $1.75 billion of unsecured notes issuances in March 2025. We delivered a solid start to 2026, with portfolio performance in line with expectations as we executed on our strategic priorities, said Alan Liu, chief executive officer. We advanced our repowering program, completing approximately 30% of our planned 2026 repowerings to date. We also advanced our battery storage co-investment with NextEra Energy Resources, electing to participate with a 49% expected interest in each of the four projects, which are expected to add approximately 200 net megawatts of battery storage capacity to our portfolio by year-end 2027. Looking ahead, we remain focused on simplifying our capital structure and allocating capital into attractive, value-enhancing investments within our existing asset base. Financial expectations For calendar year 2026, XPLR Infrastructure continues to expect the portfolio to deliver adjusted EBITDA of $1.75 billion to $1.95 billion and FCFBG of $600 million to $700 million.
