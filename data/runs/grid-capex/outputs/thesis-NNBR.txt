# NNBR: Retooling auto capacity into the electrical grid capex supercycle

## Bear case first
The grid thesis rests on a presentation target, not on orders. Current revenue is high-volume auto, HVAC, and diesel components (1); management says it will wean the lower-margin legacy auto business as contracts expire (0). The Electrical Grid & Data Center plan has no named customers, no federal awards (USASpending), and no dated milestones; the only timeline is a 2029-2030 sales goal (0). Insiders sold $45.3K and bought nothing (Form 4); the share count is up 64.2% with a shelf on file (reality check).

## Business today
NN manufactures high-volume precision components for power steering, braking, transmissions, gasoline fuel systems, HVAC, and diesel systems (1). Revenue was $422.2M and cash was $11.4M as of 2025-12-31 (SEC XBRL). Management is retooling equipment toward Electrical Grid & Data Center, Defense & Electronics, Medical, and High-Value Vehicle (0).

## Chain position
The mapped position is first-order: a component supplier into high-voltage transformer, switchgear, and circuit breaker manufacturers. Utility procurement for data center load pushes backlogs past two years, which firms pricing and flows volume to suppliers. NN's current grid revenue is not disclosed; its place in the chain rests on the stated growth plan (0), not on confirmed backlog.

## Customer graph
Government buyers: $0 in federal awards (USASpending). Enterprise buyers: none named; 3 other filers name the company, but no purchasing relationship is identified. The grid plan is unproven at the customer level; legacy customers are described only by application (1). Customer evidence is thin.

## Insider and management signal
Net open-market insider activity is $-45.3K from 1 sale and 0 buys (Form 4). Management language is quantified: $80M to $100M of new business growth per year, a $422M to $600M sales goal, and explicit equipment retooling (0). The signals conflict: no insider buying, committed targets.

## Street view
Sell-side coverage is unknown: analyst count, consensus, and mean target are n/a, and no firm rating actions are visible. Thin coverage is part of the set-up but means no independent validation of the plan.

## Non-linear case
The path to a multiple of today's market cap is a margin mix shift: grid and data center components at higher margins replacing the legacy auto book, with revenue reaching the $600M target (0). That requires NN to convert retooled capacity into purchase orders from transformer or switchgear OEMs and to sustain $80M to $100M of new business growth per year (0). Pricing power is the non-linear element; long lead times let suppliers push price rather than accept auto-like terms. The $301M market cap (reality check) embeds none of the transition beyond the investor presentation (0).

## Evidence
- Management plans to add $80M to $100M of new business growth per year, wean lower-margin legacy auto, and retool equipment (0).
- Long-term goal: $422M sales in 2025 to $600M by 2029-2030 (0).
- Growth areas named: Electrical Grid & Data Center, Defense & Electronics, Medical, High-Value Vehicle (0).
- Current business: high-volume components for power steering, braking, transmissions, gasoline fuel systems, HVAC, diesel injection, and diesel emissions (1).

## Sizing and survivability
Average daily dollar volume is $13.2M, so a small fund needs 19 trading days to build a position (reality check). Cash is $11.4M against net cash of -$145.7M (SEC XBRL; reality check), and the share count is up 64.2% with a shelf on file (reality check). Dilution risk is material; the negative net cash position is the key survivability constraint.

## What would change our mind
- An open-market insider buy on a Form 4 would reverse the selling signal.
- A federal award from DOE or DoD visible in USASpending would add a government customer.
- A 10-Q or 10-K with a named grid customer or quantified backlog would move the thesis from presentation to proof.
