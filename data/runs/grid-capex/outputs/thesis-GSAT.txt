# GSAT: Satellite IoT operator with no grid or data center exposure, thin customer evidence, and heavy insider selling

## Bear case first

GSAT's existing satellite MSS and IoT services [2] produced $273.0M in revenue, and the company holds $447.5M cash (SEC XBRL), so the data center interconnection theme is not needed to keep it solvent. Federal awards, while small at $1.9M (USASpending), confirm government buyers. A short also faces $99.1M average daily dollar volume (reality check), which can squeeze. Yet insiders sold $30.0M with zero buys (Form 4), no named enterprise customer appears in the excerpts, and the filings contain no data center or grid interconnection program, capex, or revenue line. The $1.1B market cap therefore rests on a theme the filings never mention.

## Business today

Globalstar sells Commercial IoT, SPOT, and Duplex MSS services [2], plus connectivity for areas not served by terrestrial networks [1]. Revenue is $273.0M and cash is $447.5M (SEC XBRL); net cash is negative at $36.3M, implying debt of about $483.8M (reality check).

## Chain position

The map positions this name at an at-risk node: data center developers awaiting grid interconnection. GSAT does not occupy that node on the evidence in the filings, which describe satellite IoT and MSS services [2] and terrestrial backup use cases [1]. No filing link connects its revenue to data center load, grid study capacity, or network upgrade fees.

## Customer graph

Government agencies pay GSAT: $538.8K from DOJ, $175.2K from DoD, and $158.7K from DHS, totaling $1.9M (USASpending). No named enterprise customer appears in the excerpts. Four other SEC filers name GSAT (customer graph), but the filings do not quantify commercial revenue from them. Customer evidence is thin against $273.0M revenue (SEC XBRL).

## Insider and management signal

Insiders sold $30.0M net with zero buys and 50 sales over the trailing 12 months (Form 4). Management language in the 10-K is descriptive: satellite IoT demand is growing [3], LEO systems have a latency advantage [4], but no data center or grid program, capex target, or quantified goal appears. The sale cluster reads as low conviction.

## Street view

No sell-side analyst coverage, consensus, or mean target is visible in the available data (street view). The lack of coverage leaves the stock open to thematic narrative without analyst pushback.

## Non-linear case

The downside path is a repricing of the implied data center premium. If the theme unwinds or GSAT reports no thematic revenue, the $1.1B market cap has no file support at about 4x revenue and negative net cash of $36.3M (reality check). A correction would be severe, and a subsequent equity raise would dilute at a low price. The downside compounds if LEO competitors take MSS share, since the 10-K concedes LEO's latency advantage [4].

## Evidence

- [1] "We provide reliable connectivity in areas not served or underserved by terrestrial wireless and wireline networks and in circumstances where terrestrial networks are not operational due to natural or man-made disasters" (10-K).
- [2] "We currently provide the following communications products and services to our MSS subscribers: ... Commercial IoT ... SPOT ... and Duplex" (10-K).
- [3] "Satellite IoT connectivity has become more critical to a growing number of sectors and use cases" (10-K).
- [4] "LEO systems reduce transmission delay compared to a geosynchronous system due to the shorter distance signals have to travel" (10-K).

## Sizing and survivability

A small fund can build a position in about 3 trading days at $99.1M average daily dollar volume (reality check). Net cash is negative at $36.3M (reality check), so the balance sheet does not fund a long wait. The liquidity cuts both ways: it enables position building, but can also squeeze a short.

## What would change our mind

1. A Form 4 with open-market insider buys. 2. An 8-K or 10-K naming a data center or grid interconnection customer, program, or revenue line. 3. A USASpending federal award above $50M tied to grid or data center services.
