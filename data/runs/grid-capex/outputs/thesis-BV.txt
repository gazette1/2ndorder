# BV: No data center gas generation aftermarket exposure; thesis fails on filing evidence

## Bear case first
A disconfirming reading of the evidence is that BrightView is a landscape maintenance and development contractor. Every revenue line in the quarterly filing comes from Maintenance Services or Development Services, with no mention of gas turbines, engine aftermarket, or data center load [0][2][7]. The dossier shows no insider buying, only $273.95K in federal awards, and negative net cash. The filing cannot support a claim that the US grid capex supercycle benefits this company.

## Business today
BrightView sells recurring landscape maintenance, snow removal, and development services to Fortune 500 corporate campuses, HOAs, public parks, airports, municipalities, hospitals, schools, restaurants, retail, and golf courses [2][16]. The 10-Q reports net service revenues of $717.6M for the three months ended June 30, 2026 [7]. The dossier lists no XBRL revenue as of 2025-09-30 and reports cash of $74.5M (SEC XBRL).

## Chain position
BrightView occupies no node in the data center gas generation adoption chain. The thesis expected gas turbine and engine service and aftermarket providers, but the filing describes only landscape maintenance and development services [0][2][13]. No economic logic connects route-based landscape services to turbine overhaul.

A non-linear outcome would require BrightView to transform into a gas generation services provider. That would appear as new reportable segments, named contracts, or related revenue in 10-K or 10-Q filings, none of which are present [0][7][13]. Without that evidence, a path to a multiple of today's business is not supported.
