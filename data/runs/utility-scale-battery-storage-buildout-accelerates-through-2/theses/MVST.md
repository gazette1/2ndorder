## Business today
Microvast sells 290Ah LFP cell-based battery packs for commercial and heavy-duty industrial applications [0]. It also describes LFP as a cost-effective cathode material widely used in commercial vehicle and ESS applications [1]. Revenue was $427.5M and cash was $105.0M at 2025-12-31 (XBRL).

## Chain position
Microvast occupies the LFP battery pack and cathode material node. The economic logic is that utility-scale projects specify lower-cost, longer-life LFP, and Microvast already makes LFP packs [0] for an ESS use case [1]. Large repetitive utility procurements can concentrate volume among cathode suppliers, but the excerpts do not quantify Microvast's cathode powder sales or utility-scale exposure.

## Non-linear case
The non-linear case is one large utility design win that turns Microvast's existing LFP pack line [0] into repeatable, high-volume revenue. That requires a named customer, qualification, or multi-year supply agreement in a future filing. If LFP remains the specified grid chemistry and precursor supply tightens, cathode pricing can improve, and Microvast could benefit from its LFP-based product line [0]. The upside multiple depends on capacity and contracted demand, neither of which appears in the excerpts.
