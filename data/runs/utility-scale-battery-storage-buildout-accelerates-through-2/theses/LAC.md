The only projected revenue stream is lithium carbonate [4], and lithium chemicals are cyclical [17]. Battery-grade failure could cut realized prices [16]. Filings show no completed facility, no revenue, and no buyer beyond GM.

## Business today
Revenue is not reported; cash was $568.226M (SEC XBRL). Thacker Pass is planned to sell battery-quality lithium carbonate to GM under a Phase 1 offtake agreement [10], with Phase 1 capacity of 40,000 t/y and mechanical completion targeted for late 2027 [1]. The only revenue stream is lithium carbonate [4].

## Chain position
The company occupies the lithium carbonate refining node, not the cathode node [4]. Higher LFP cathode output pulls more lithium chemicals through supply chains; cathode plants are the primary industrial buyers. Utility storage acceleration tightens contract pricing at refiners before volume reaches mining companies.

## Non-linear case
At a $1.008B market cap, the multiple path depends on project scale and tighter lithium pricing. The Base Case projects 135,132 t/y and $3.2B annual revenue [7]. Each phase is 40,000 t/y [5]; Phases 2-4 require approval [2]. The outcome requires commercial-scale operation of a novel process [14] and prices near the $24K/t forecast [6].

## Evidence

- Phases 2-4 add 40,000 t/y each if approved [2]; Phases 1-4 are each designed for 40,000 t/y [5].
- OPEX estimate is $8.039K/t over the mine life and $6.238K/t in Years 1-25 [3].
- The only revenue stream is lithium carbonate [4].
- The study forecasts a $24K/t lithium carbonate selling price [6].
- Base Case annual average production is 135,132 t/y and annual average revenue is $3.2B [7].

- The novel process has not been demonstrated at commercial scale [14].
- Battery-grade quality failure could reduce realized prices [16].
- Lithium chemicals are subject to commercial and commodity price cycles [17].
