The filings warn the process has not been demonstrated at commercial scale [14]. The dossier shows 0 buys and 0 sales from insiders in the trailing 12 months (Form 4). ## Non-linear case

The non-linear outcome requires utility storage demand to tighten lithium chemical contracts before Thacker Pass supplies. The base case assumes a $24,000/t lithium carbonate price [6], average annual revenue of $3,200MM [7], and four phases of 40,000 t/y [5]. If those prices hold and the novel process reaches commercial scale [14], revenue could be a multiple of today's market capitalization. A slip past late 2027 [1] would break the sequencing.
