## Business today
Energy Vault sells B-VAULT electrochemical BESS systems for one-to-four-hour storage [11], plus system design, integration, software controls, and lifecycle services [16]. Its Own & Operate segment has Cross Trails, 57 MW / 114 MWh, in commercial operation with a 10-year Gridmatic offtake [2][3], and a consent and waiver to the Cross Trails credit agreement was filed on 2026-07-01 [0]. Revenue was $203.7MM and cash was $58.3MM as of 2025-12-31 (SEC XBRL).

## Chain position
Energy Vault is a contracted utility-scale storage developer, a beneficiary of grid-operator procurement targets and interconnection queue acceleration. Signed offtakes, including the 10-year Gridmatic contract [3] and the 14-year AEMO and AusEnergy agreements [7][9], convert into construction revenue and recurring cash flows ahead of merchant exposure.

## Customer graph
Gridmatic, an AI-enabled power marketer, is an enterprise buyer under a 10-year offtake [3]. AEMO Services and AusEnergy Services are government buyers under 14-year agreements [7][9]. Federal awards are $0 (USASpending), one other filer names the company (SEC XBRL), and the set is thin: two government buyers, one enterprise.

## Non-linear case
The path to a multiple is Own & Operate scaling from Cross Trails to Stoney Creek, SOSA, and Ebor [2][6][7][8][9], turning long-duration contracts into recurring cash flows. Each additional contracted project acquired under Asset Vault [15] extends the portfolio without merchant price exposure. That requires development, financing, and construction execution on all pipeline projects, plus continued procurement wins in ERCOT, CAISO, and NSW.
