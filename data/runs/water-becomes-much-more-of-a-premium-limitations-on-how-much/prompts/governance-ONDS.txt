If your shares are held by a brokerage firm, bank, or a trustee, you should provide proof of beneficial ownership as of the Record Date, such as a bank or brokerage account statement or other similar evidence of ownership. Each stockholder of Common Stock is entitled to one vote per share on each of the four director nominees and one vote per share on each other matter properly presented at the Annual Meeting for each share of Common Stock owned by that stockholder on the Record Date. What constitutes a quorum? The holders of a majority of the shares of stock, issued and outstanding and entitled to vote, shall be present in person or represented by proxy in order to constitute a quorum for the Annual Meeting.
