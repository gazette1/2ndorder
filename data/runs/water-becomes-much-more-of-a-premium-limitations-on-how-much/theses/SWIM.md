## Business today
Latham sells fiberglass in-ground pools and related residential pool products, benefiting from conversion away from concrete and legacy materials. [0][1][3] Homeowners are the end customer. Revenue was $545.912MM and cash was $71.043MM as of 2025-12-31 (SEC XBRL).

## Non-linear case
Downside compounds if water restrictions move from drought emergencies to permanent monthly purchase caps. A homeowner who cannot fill a pool cancels at contract signing. Existing owners reduce fill and chemical service frequency, cutting route density. Revenue declines against a net cash position of -$208.798MM (Reality check), turning an earnings miss into a balance-sheet problem. The equity outcome is a fraction of today's $629MM market cap.
