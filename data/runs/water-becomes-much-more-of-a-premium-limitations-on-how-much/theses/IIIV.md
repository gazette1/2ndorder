Cash was $66.7M as of September 30, 2025 (SEC XBRL). On April 1, 2025, it acquired a utility billing software company to expand public sector offerings [0][2].

## Chain position
Third-party utility billing and meter data management software vendor. The economic logic is that AMI deployments generate recurring demand for interval billing engines and usage-alert platforms, and rationing rules require dynamic tariffs that legacy on-premise suites cannot support. Configurable cloud platforms win multi-year replacement contracts.

## Non-linear case
If water rationing becomes a regulatory mandate, utilities must deploy tiered rates, cap notifications, and real-time usage alerts. Legacy billing systems cannot support these. Each replacement is a multi-year implementation contract with recurring software revenue. IIIV's acquisition, though small, could be the wedge into this market. The outcome becomes a multiple of today's business if the acquisition is expanded with further investment in cloud-based configurable platforms. The evidence does not show that investment yet.
