Business today

That dynamic expands a small installed base for equipment suppliers before conventional utilities see revenue shifts.

The economic logic is that scarcity raises the value of integrated water and recycled water services, but utility revenue is still regulated and tied to allowed rates, so tariff risk and volume risk sit inside the model [0][1].
