# ARTESIAN RESOURCES CORP: Rainwater harvesting is a demand threat, not a filed growth segment

## Bear case first
A disconfirming reading is direct: drought restrictions reduce water sales [6]. Management describes drought only as a risk [4][5]. No filing shows installation revenue, named programs, or capital commitments for rainwater harvesting in [0]-[6]. Insider selling and $0 federal awards add no validation (Form 4, USASpending).

## Business today
Artesian is a regulated water utility serving customers in Pennsylvania and Delaware, with PADEP and DRBC oversight [2][3]. It received $10.0M in Superfund trust reimbursements [0] and has a three-year tank painting agreement [1]. Revenue was $112.941M and cash was $52K (SEC XBRL).

## Chain position
Artesian sits at the capped-utility node, not the installer node. Restrictions reduce demand for its water [6], while installers capture substitution. No filing places Artesian in gutter, cistern, pump, or filtration installation.

## Non-linear case
For a non-linear outcome, Artesian would need to become a rainwater harvesting installer or infrastructure owner. That requires named contracts, capacity additions, permits, and revenue from tanks, pumps, or filtration. None appears in [0]-[6]. Without it, the chain leaves Artesian with lower unit sales and fixed costs.
