## Business today
Itron sells Industrial Internet of Things solutions, including automated meter reading and advanced metering infrastructure for electricity, water, and gas, plus distributed energy resource management, grid edge devices, distribution automation communications, smart lighting, and smart city sensors [0][1]. Revenue was $2,367.2M and cash was $1,020.4M as of 2025-12-31 (SEC XBRL).

## Chain position
Itron is an order 1 beneficiary, a meter vendor and systems integrator at the first node of a rationing-driven adoption chain. Utilities facing household water limits must deploy AMI to monitor usage, bill against caps, and enforce compliance. Rationing converts metering from a billing convenience into a regulatory necessity, which can accelerate adoption even among utilities that previously delayed upgrades.

## Non-linear case
The path to a multiple of today's business requires water rationing rules that force utilities to buy and deploy AMI meters on a fixed compliance timeline. If rationing becomes widespread, meter orders could arrive in a lumpy, award-driven wave rather than a gradual replacement curve [0][1]. Itron already sells water AMI, so it can capture demand without new development. What has to be true: regulators enact enforceable household limits, utilities are held to those limits, and Itron wins a share of the resulting contracts.
